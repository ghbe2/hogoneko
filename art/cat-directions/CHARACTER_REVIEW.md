## 最終生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original rescue-cat mobile game art direction comparison sheet.
Image 1 is our previous flat cat sheet, a STYLE STARTING POINT, not an identity to preserve. User says it is too generic and needs more quirk. Create three distinctly authored character-design directions with feline attitude, not merely new coat colors or expressions on identical heads.
Keep the simple flat colored shapes, believable feline seated anatomy, sparse fine whiskers and modular broad coat masks of the reference. REMOVE its airbrushed shading: genuinely solid vector-like fills, pure white background, no texture, no gradients, no shadows.
Composition: landscape sheet, 3 columns headed only "F", "G", "H"; 2 rows headed only "ADULT", "KITTEN". Six full-body cats, adult above matching kitten, lots of breathing room, no cropped tails. Each column is a different coherent design language, NOT progressively worse anatomy. All cats are endearing real cats, never humans dressed as cats.
F: aloof sly elegance. A horizontally wide angular cheek silhouette, high small pointed ears, narrow half-lidded golden eyes glancing sideways, tiny low-set straight mouth, slender pear-shaped seated torso, unexpectedly thick hooked tail. Small asymmetric ear angle and one forepaw slightly ahead. Brown tabby broad sparse stripes, white off-center chin patch. Kitten retains angular personality but shorter legs/torso and larger head ratio.
G: charmingly blank and offbeat. Broad flattened oval face, wide-set modest round eyes with small vertical pupils, tiny close-set nose and mouth low on face, slightly uneven cheek contours. A compact bottom-heavy cat body, relaxed shoulders, mildly off-center seated posture, not a round plush blob. Tortoiseshell black/orange body with distinct calico white/black/orange short tail. An understated puzzled little cat, no big smile or human eyebrows. Kitten clearly has baby proportions, not a scaled adult.
H: scruffy dignified street-cat charisma, not injured. More square muzzle, long narrow eyes and a deadpan unamused gaze, cheek outline with just two broad blunt tufts, tall ears with slightly different tilts, lean sturdy body, big simple feline paws. Black-and-white mask asymmetric naturally; adult has NO TAIL, clean rump with no nub; kitten has a short bent tail. A subtle slouch and weight shift, not aggressive, ugly or monsterlike.
Constraints: quirks should read in SILHOUETTE AND FACE even with coat hidden. Cat eyes not human eyes: colored iris, dark slit pupil, no human whites, no eyebrows, no eyelashes, no sparkle highlights. Avoid identical generic smiling emoji faces, huge Disney eyes, 3D, painterly fur, over-cute chibi bodies, accessories, clothes, props, watermark. Broad modular patterns easy to swap across head, torso, legs, tail. Adult and kitten skeletons distinct. Original designs.
