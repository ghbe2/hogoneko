# フラットな猫の候補 D / E

参考画像は画風の参考のみ。個別の猫や透かしは複製しない。
D は細身、E は少し丸い形。上段は成猫、下段は子猫。
中央は錆柄の胴体＋三毛の尾。右上は尾なし、右下は短尾。

これは方向性確認用の一枚絵で、分離済みのパーツ素材ではない。
採用後に頭・胴体・脚・尾と柄マスクを分離する。

### D

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original production-direction concept sheet for a rescue-cat mobile game, raster illustration with a very clean VECTOR-LIKE appearance.
Reference image: supplied image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Study its economical silhouette, flat solid fills, large simple colored eyes, minimal linework, pointed pink ears, upright seated CAT posture, long front legs and small curved mouth. Create ORIGINAL cat designs and a NEW layout; do not copy the individual cats or source layout. Do not reproduce text, logos, watermark, borders or diagonal lines.
CRITICAL: FLAT 2D GRAPHIC, NOT painted, NOT realistic fur, NOT shaded volume, NOT Disney, NOT anime rendering, NOT 3D. Zero gradients, zero ground shadows, zero individual fur strands. Only 4–6 solid colors per cat plus eyes. Eyes are simple colored ovals with black vertical pupils, no sparkle blobs. Fine restrained whiskers. Cat anatomy remains convincing within simplification, not human posture, not a plush ball.
Layout: pure white wide sheet, six original full-body seated cats, two rows of three, comfortable whitespace, consistent front-facing view, feet aligned, full ears and tails in frame. Tiny labels: "ADULT" upper row and "KITTEN" lower row, no other text except style letter.
Upper row: adult brown tabby with a few broad dark stripes and long curling tail; adult with BLACK-ORANGE TORTOISESHELL TORSO and distinct WHITE-BLACK-ORANGE CALICO TAIL (intentional modular coat mix); adult black and white cat with NO TAIL at all, smooth rump, no nub, no tail behind legs.
Lower row: corresponding kittens with a distinctly shorter torso and limbs, larger head/body ratio and ears, small muzzle, NOT simply scaled-down adults. Left tabby long tail, middle tortoiseshell body + calico tail, right black/white BOBTAIL with one short curved tail. Pattern islands are broad clean shapes, interchangeable head/torso/legs/tail regions; no sewn patchwork seams.
Aim at elegant, friendly Japanese flat animal illustration readable in a small game scene.
Direction D: slender and elegant, closest to reference. Adult long slim front legs, small softly triangular head, vertically elongated seated silhouette, almond-oval eyes, thin unobtrusive dark contour only at key overlaps. Label "D".
